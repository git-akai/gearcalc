4340 Hardened Steel  [AISI 4340 (UNS G43400)]
  condition: oil quenched from 845 °C, tempered at 425 °C — 46 HRC
  source:    ASM Desk Edition tempering table for oil-quenched 4340, as reproduced by theworldmaterial.com. Retrieved 2026-08-15.
  ultimate  Tensile yield strength at this temper. UTS is 1500 MPa.
  fatigue   0.5 × UTS, the classical steel endurance ratio, which reproduces the annealed entry's published 330 MPa to within 5 %. No published fatigue figure was found at THIS temper. Treat as optimistic: the endurance ratio is known to fall away above roughly 1400 MPa UTS rather than track 0.5 indefinitely, so the true figure is plausibly nearer 700 MPa. The first number here worth replacing with your own data.

  source:    Copper Development Association alloy data for C36000, as published by National Bronze. Retrieved 2026-08-15.

  modulus   14 000 ksi.
